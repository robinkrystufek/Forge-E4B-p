Write a coherent scene of about 400 words from the mission notes below. Use plain English

and what remains unexplained. Use only these notes as the source of events and names.

The survey ship Ardent arrives at Halcyon Relay just before the station passes into the
shadow of its planet. The exterior navigation lights still work, but a docking beacon
pulses once every forty seconds instead of once every four seconds. The crew approaches
from the sunlit side and spots a rectangular repair patch near the main antenna. It looks
newer than the surrounding hull. The station sends no reply to three radio calls.

Mara and Ivo dock at the maintenance collar. The airlock opens without resistance. Inside,
two pressure gauges disagree. Mara checks a portable gauge against the airlock panel and
finds that the station is holding pressure. She keeps their suits sealed until they can
check the next compartment. Ivo finds a handwritten note under a transparent cover. The
note asks the next shift to leave the battery circuit connected overnight.

In the equipment room, a narrow channel on a printed wiring diagram connects the beacon
and the recording cabinet. An amber light on that channel blinks when the beacon pulses.
The cabinet holds a record of the last six hours. It shows a steady rise in power draw
beginning shortly after the antenna was repaired. The last entry marks a manual change
to the station clock. The record contains no entry identifying who changed it.

Mara wants to disconnect the beacon to stop the power drain. Ivo points out that the crew
may need the beacon to find the collar again on the way back. They agree to isolate the
recording cabinet first and copy its data to the ship. Once the copy is verified, Mara
sends a status update to the pilot and asks her to watch the beacon from outside.
Just as the station enters shadow, the beacon flashes twice. The pilot confirms the
double flash before the signal goes quiet. End the scene with the crew deciding how to
investigate the unexpected flash while keeping their return route open.
